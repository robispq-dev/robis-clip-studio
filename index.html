Features

Familiar timeline editor

Built for 9:16

Vietnamese text done right

Titles and SRT subtitles render with full Vietnamese diacritics; the exported video matches the preview exactly. Use your own fonts.

Brand templates

Save colors, logo, fonts and subtitle position as reusable templates for consistent branding.

MP4 export

Post & schedule

Write separate captions per platform, post now or schedule (Vietnam time), and track the status of each post.

How it works

  1. Install ROBIS Clip Studio on Windows 10/11 and open it.
  2. Drop your clips into the editor, add titles, subtitles, logo and music, then export an MP4.
  3. In Settings → Accounts, click Connect TikTok account. TikTok's own login page opens in your browser; you sign in and approve access.
  4. In Post & schedule, pick the video and your account, write the caption, choose who can see the video and interaction settings, confirm TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation, then post now or schedule it.
  5. The app uploads the video to TikTok and shows the status until TikTok confirms the post.

TikTok integration

TikTok product / scopeWhat the app uses it for
Login Kit — user.info.basicLets the user connect their own TikTok account and shows the account's display name and avatar so the user knows where the video will go.
Content Posting API — video.publishDirect Post: uploads the user's edited video to their own profile with the privacy level, interaction settings, caption and commercial-content disclosure the user chose.
Content Posting API — video.uploadSend-to-inbox: uploads the video as a draft so the user can finish editing and post it inside the TikTok app.

The app never posts without the user's explicit action, never adds its own watermark or promotional text, and shows TikTok's creator settings (privacy options, disabled comments/duet/stitch, maximum duration) before every post.

FAQ

Who is it for? Small businesses, online sellers and creators who make short vertical videos and want to post them to their own TikTok and Facebook accounts.

Where is my data stored? Projects, videos and account tokens stay on your computer. Tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). See the Privacy Policy.

Can I disconnect my TikTok account? Yes — remove it in Settings, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Does scheduling work when my computer is off? TikTok posts are sent by the app, so the computer must be on and online at the scheduled time. Facebook Reels can be scheduled on Facebook's servers.
